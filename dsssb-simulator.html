DSSSB Typing Test Guidelines (LDC, JSA, DEO)

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) conducts typing skill tests as a qualifying stage for various clerical posts. Candidates are required to clear the prescribed speed limits on a computer keyboard.

Speed Requirements

  • English Typing: Minimum 35 Words Per Minute (WPM).
  • Hindi Typing: Minimum 30 Words Per Minute (WPM).
  • Tests are generally of 10 minutes duration.

Font & Keyboard Layouts

For Hindi typing, DSSSB usually permits candidates to choose their familiar layout prior to the exam. Popular options include:

  • Kruti Dev / Devlys: Traditional Remington typewriter layout.
  • Mangal (Inscript): The standard modern Unicode layout.
  • Mangal (Remington Gail): Unicode font typed using the old typewriter mapping.

💡 Pro Tip: Backspace rules can change depending on the specific exam notice. Practice with both "Backspace Allowed" and "Spacebar-Lock" modes to be fully prepared for any software configuration the board uses.
