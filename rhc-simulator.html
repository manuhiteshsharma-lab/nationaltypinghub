Rajasthan High Court - LDC & Stenographer Rules

📊 Track Formats & Language Choices

Do Stenographer candidates need to qualify in both Hindi and English typing?

NO. Steno candidates are tested strictly in the single language option selected during application. Your shorthand script and your 10-minute computer typing test will match that language only.

What is the structure of the Clerk Grade-II (LDC) / JJA Track?

LDC and JJA candidates must take a dual-language speed test. It consists of 5 minutes of English typing and 5 minutes of Hindi typing sequentially.

🔤 Font, Layout & System Restraints

Is Mangal font or the standard Inscript keyboard allowed for Hindi in RHC?

NO. Guidelines strictly restrict Hindi input to KrutiDev 010 using a Remington Gail layout. Mangal and Inscript layouts do not work on these terminals.

Am I allowed to use the backspace key to fix text typing errors?

YES, but only within the active word. Once you press the Spacebar to begin the next word, the previous word is locked. You cannot jump back lines or sentences to change typos. This is known as Spacebar-Lock.

Will misspelled words be highlighted during the test?

NO. The test application has NO word highlighting, NO spellcheck underline, and NO visual auto-corrections active. It is a completely blind typing experience. You can practice this by checking the Strict Exam Mode option before starting.

🧮 Mistake Calculation & Re-looping

How are typing mistakes processed to calculate my net score?

The evaluation program logs full and partial mistakes.
Full Mistakes: Omitted words, misspellings, substitutions.
Partial Mistakes: Punctuation misses, wrong spacing, incorrect case.
Rule: 2 Partial Mistakes = 1 Full Mistake.

Net Keystrokes = Gross Keystrokes - Full Mistakes - (Partials / 2)

What should I do if I finish typing the provided passage early?

If you complete the paragraph before the time limit, you must immediately restart typing the text from the beginning. This allows you to accumulate extra gross keystrokes to maximize your scoring potential. Our simulator handles this automatically.

What is the minimum speed threshold for RHC typing?

Both LDC and Stenographer tracks demand a minimum threshold of 8,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH). This translates to approximately 26.67 WPM. You must clear minimum pass marks (22.5 for General, 20.0 for SC/ST/PH) out of the 50 total allotted speed test marks.
