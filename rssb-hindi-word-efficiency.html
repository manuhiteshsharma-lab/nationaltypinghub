RSSB LDC & Junior Assistant 2026 Hindi Efficiency Test (MS Word 2013) Guide

As per the official Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) Phase-II notification, candidates who clear Phase-I must qualify four computer tests: Hindi Speed Test (25 marks), English Speed Test (25 marks), Hindi Efficiency Test in MS Word 2013 (25 marks), and English Efficiency Test in MS Excel 2013 (25 marks). Each test runs for exactly 10 minutes.

Key Exam-Day Rules Implemented in This Simulator

  • 15 Formatting Questions (25 Marks): Covers paragraph alignment, line spacing (`1.25`, `1.35`, `1.5`), left indentation, Find & Replace formatting, headers, footers, page numbers, diagonal watermarks, page borders, footnotes, drop cap, multi-column layouts, and table shading/merging/borders.
  • Strict Ribbon-Only Interface: Cut, Copy, Paste, Select All, Right-Click, and Function Keys are disabled. All tasks must be performed via mouse clicks on the top Ribbon toolbar.
  • Undo (`Ctrl+Z`) Permitted: Per the 2026 RSSB guidelines, candidates can use `Ctrl+Z` to undo accidental formatting changes without losing marks.
  • Minimum Qualifying Marks: Candidates must score at least 9 out of 25 marks in each paper (with up to 5% relaxation for SC/ST candidates).
