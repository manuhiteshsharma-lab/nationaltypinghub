Official Typing Speed & Marks Formulas Across Indian Government Exams

Different recruitment boards in India evaluate typing skill tests using distinct mathematical formulas. Some boards evaluate Actual Words Typed (like RSSB LDC 2026), while others standardize word length using 1 Word = 5 Keystrokes (Key Depressions) (like SSC, HARTRON SETC, MP CPCT, and RRB NTPC).

How to Convert Between KDPH (Key Depressions Per Hour) and WPM

  • Strokes to Standard Words: Divide your total keystrokes (including spaces) by 5. For example, typing 1,750 keystrokes in 10 minutes equals 350 standard words.
  • Keystrokes to KDPH: Multiply your strokes per minute by 60. For example, 2,000 keystrokes in a 15-minute SSC test equals (2000 ÷ 15) × 60 = 8,000 KDPH.
  • KDPH to WPM: Divide KDPH by 300 (since 60 minutes × 5 strokes = 300). Thus, 8,000 KDPH = 26.67 WPM and 10,500 KDPH = 35 WPM.
