Easy Guide: Clear RSSB Junior Assistant & LDC Phase 2 Exam

Getting a government job as a Junior Assistant (JA) or Lower Division Clerk (LDC) in Rajasthan is a dream for many students. First, you have to pass Phase 1 (the written exam). But the real challenge is Phase 2.

Phase 2 is the Typing and Efficiency Test. Many students fail here because they do not know the correct rules or they make simple formatting mistakes. In this guide, we use simple words to explain exactly how Phase 2 works and how you can easily pass it.

Phase 2 Exam Pattern (How It Works)

The Phase 2 exam is done completely on a computer. You will have to take tests in two languages: Hindi and English.

  • Hindi Typing Speed Test: 10 Minutes (25 Marks)
  • Hindi Efficiency Test: 10 Minutes (25 Marks)
  • English Typing Speed Test: 10 Minutes (25 Marks)
  • English Efficiency Test: 10 Minutes (25 Marks)

⚠️ Important Rule: You must get a minimum of 9 marks in each of these four tests to pass. If you get less than 9 marks in even one test, you will be disqualified.

How to Pass the Typing Speed Test

To pass the speed test, you should have a good typing speed without making mistakes. In Rajasthan RSSB exams, the Hindi typing test is officially taken using the Devlys 010 font. This font requires practice because Accuracy is must.As per new guidelines In typing special characters use Alt-key combinations not needed.

  • Practice Without Looking: Learn to type while looking at the screen, not your keyboard. This is called touch typing.
  • Focus on Accuracy: Do not just try to type fast. If you type wrong words, your marks will go down quickly.
  • Take Mock Tests: Practice every day for 10 minutes continuously using our simulator to build your stamina.
