RRB NTPC Typing Test Rules 2026: The 5% Mistake Formula Explained

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) typing skill test is one of the most strictly evaluated exams in India. Unlike simple WPM calculators, RRB uses a highly specific mathematical formula to penalize errors. If you do not understand this formula, you risk failing the exam even if your typing speed feels fast.

Exam Parameters & Requirements

  • Duration: Exactly 10 minutes.
  • English Typing: Minimum 30 WPM required.
  • Hindi Typing: Minimum 25 WPM required. You can choose either Kruti Dev or Mangal font.
  • Tools Disabled: Editing tools, spelling checks, and often the Backspace key are completely disabled during the test.

💡 What is the 5% Mistake Exemption?
RRB allows you to make mistakes up to 5% of the total words you typed. These mistakes are ignored. However, for every mistake you make above that 5% limit, 10 words will be deducted from your total typed words.

The Official Calculation Formula

The RRB evaluation software calculates your final speed in the following steps:

  1. Total Strokes Typed: Counted by the system (e.g., 1600 strokes).
  2. Total Words: Total Strokes divided by 5 (e.g., 1600 / 5 = 320 words).
  3. Ignorable Mistakes: 5% of Total Words (e.g., 5% of 320 = 16 mistakes ignored).
  4. Penalty: (Total Mistakes made - Ignorable Mistakes) × 10.
  5. Net Speed: (Total Words - Penalty) / Time in minutes.

How to Clear the Test

Because the penalty is so severe (1 mistake = 10 words lost), your primary focus must be Accuracy. It is far better to type slightly slower with 98% accuracy than to type extremely fast with 90% accuracy.
