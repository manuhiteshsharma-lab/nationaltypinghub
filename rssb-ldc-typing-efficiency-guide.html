3. Exact Marks Calculation Formula (Speed Test का असली गणित)

Pichhle saalon tak students mein confusion rehta tha ki RSSB keystrokes ginta hai ya words, aur penalty kaise kat-ti hai. Is baar 2026 ke official notification mein RSSB ne 100% crystal clear mathematical formula de diya hai. Chaliye isko detail mein samajhte hain:

A. English Typing Speed Test Formula (500 Words Paragraph)

  • Aapko computer screen par 500 Words (शब्दों) ka ek English paragraph dikhaya jayega.
  • Time milega: 10 Minutes (Yaani poora paragraph khatam karne ke liye aapko 50 WPM ki speed chahiye).
  • Har 1 Sahi Word (Correct Word) type karne par aapko 0.05 Marks milenge.
  • Galat word, chhoote hue word, ya extra word ke 0 Marks milenge (unke marks nahi diye jayenge).
📊 English Typing Marks Formula: English Marks = (Total Correct Words Typed) × 0.05

Example: Agar aapne 10 minute mein 420 words type kiye, jisme 20 words galat hain, toh Sahi Words = 400.
Aapke Marks = 400 × 0.05 = 20.00 Marks (Out of 25)

B. Hindi Typing Speed Test Formula (400 Words Paragraph)

  • Aapko computer screen par 400 Words (शब्दों) ka ek Hindi paragraph (DevLys 010 font mein) dikhaya jayega.
  • Time milega: 10 Minutes (Yaani poora paragraph khatam karne ke liye aapko 40 WPM ki speed chahiye).
  • Har 1 Sahi Word (Correct Word) type karne par aapko 0.0625 Marks milenge.
📊 Hindi Typing Marks Formula: Hindi Marks = (Total Correct Words Typed) × 0.0625

Example: Agar aapne 10 minute mein 340 words type kiye, jisme 20 words galat hain, toh Sahi Words = 320.
Aapke Marks = 320 × 0.0625 = 20.00 Marks (Out of 25)

4. RSSB Speed Test के 8 खतरनाक नियम (Hidden Traps in Notification)

Official PDF ke Page 1, 2 aur 3 par RSSB ne evaluation ke kuch aise kade niyam likhe hain jinhe 90% students ignore kar dete hain. Inhe dhyan se padhein:

  1. Invisible Matra Repetition Trap (मात्राओं की अनावश्यक आवृत्ति): Yeh sabse bada trap hai! DevLys 010 mein jab aap 'े' (एक मात्रा - key s) lagate hain, toh agar aapne galti se s ko do ya chaar baar daba diya, toh screen par dekhne mein woh 'केवल' hi dikhega, lekin computer ke backend code mein woh गलत ho jayega!
    • ✅ Sahi Typing: केवल = dsoy (Computer gives 0.0625 Marks)
    • ❌ Galat Typing: केवल = dsssssoy (Screen par 'केवल' hi dikhega, par computer 0 Marks dega!)
  2. Improper Character Formation Trap (वर्णों का अनुचित टंकण): Hindi DevLys 010 mein kuch akshar do keys ko jodkar bhi dikhte hain aur ek single key se bhi bante hain. RSSB ne saaf udhaharan diya hai:
    • ✅ Sahi Typing for 'प': Press single key i (Marks milenge).
    • ❌ Galat Typing for 'प': Pressing half 'प' (I) + 'ा' ki matra (k) = Ik. Yeh dekhne mein 'प' jaisa lagta hai, par computer isko 100% Galat maanega!
  3. The Spacebar Backspace-Lock Rule (बैकस्पेस कब काम करेगा?): Speed test mein agar aapne koi shabd galat type kar diya hai, toh aap Backspace daba kar use tabhi tak sudhaar (correct) sakte hain jab tak aapne agla shabd type karne ke liye Spacebar ya Enter key nahi dabayi hai. Ek baar Spacebar/Enter daba diya, toh pichhle shabd par Backspace kaam nahi karega!
  4. Alt & Ctrl Keys Strictly Banned (Alt-Code नहीं चलेंगे!): Notification ke Point No. 9 mein saaf likha hai: "परीक्षा के दौरान Alt एवं Control Key का उपयोग नहीं करें।" Iska matlab hai ki paragraph mein aise special characters nahi aayenge jinke liye Alt+Code lagana pade, aur aapko Alt ya Ctrl key bilkul nahi chhooni hai.
  5. Punctuation, Spacing & Case Sensitivity: Galat shabd/ank likhna, shabd chhodna (skip), line repeat karna, विराम चिह्नों (comma, full stop, purna viram) ka sahi prayog na karna, do shabdon ke beech double space dena, ya English mein Capital/Small letters ki galti karne par us shabd ke koi marks nahi milenge.
  6. No Personal Keyboards Allowed: Aap exam centre mein apna khud ka keyboard ya keyboard stickers nahi le ja sakte. Exam hall ke keyboard par hi typing karni hogi. Isliye ghar par practice ke liye kaunsa keyboard best rahega, yeh jaanne ke liye hamara Best Typing Keyboards for Exam Aspirants Guide zaroor padhein.

5. Complete Efficiency Test Guide: MS Word 2013 (Hindi) & MS Excel 2013 (English)

Ab aate hain sabse scoring हिस्से par! Typing speed badhane mein mahino lagte hain, lekin Efficiency Test (50 Marks = 25 Hindi + 25 English) ek aisa section hai jahan aap sirf 15-20 din ki smart practice se 50 mein se 50 Marks laa sakte hain!

Official Notification ke Section 4(ख) aur Page 5-6 ke screenshots se jo bade khulase hue hain, unhe note karein:

  • Hindi Efficiency Test: Yeh MS Word 2013 mein karwaya jayega (Font: DevLys 010 / Pre-typed text).
  • English Efficiency Test: Yeh MS Excel 2013 mein karwaya jayega! (Pichhle kuch exams mein dono Word mein hote the, par is baar official PDF mein saaf likha hai ki English Efficiency MS Excel 2013 mein hogi aur screenshot bhi Excel sheet ka diya gaya hai!).
  • Total Questions & Time: Dono bhashaon ke liye aapko exam hall mein alag se printed Question Paper diya jayega. Har paper mein 15 Questions (Tasks) honge, jinhe solve karne ke liye 10 Minutes milenge (Max Marks: 25 each).

🚨 Golden Rules of RSSB Efficiency Test (यह गलती की तो 0 मिलेंगे!)

Feature / Action Status in Efficiency Test Crucial Rule & Explanation
Cut, Copy, Paste ❌ DISABLED Na ribbon se aur na hi keyboard se Cut-Copy-Paste kaam karega.
Right Mouse Click ❌ DISABLED Mouse ka Right-Click band rahega. Sirf Left-Click se Ribbon Menu use karein.
Select All & Function Keys ❌ DISABLED Ctrl+A aur F1 se F12 tak saari Function Keys kaam nahi karengi.
Shortcut Keys ❌ BANNED Point 12: "परीक्षा में किसी शॉर्टकट की का प्रयोग नहीं करें।" Always use the top Ribbon Tabs!
CTRL + Z (Undo) & Backspace ✅ ALLOWED Agar koi galat formatting ho jaye, toh aap CTRL+Z ya Backspace daba kar use sudhaar sakte hain!
Modifying Default Text/Table ❌ ZERO MARKS PENALTY Screen par pehle se मौजूद (By Default) Paragraph ya Table ke text ko bina poochhe Delete ya Change kiya, toh us poore paragraph/table par kiye gaye kisi bhi sahi task ke marks nahi milenge!
Extra Formatting ❌ ZERO MARKS PENALTY Question mein jitni formatting poochhi gayi hai, sirf utni hi karein. Apni taraf se extra bold/color karne par us question ke marks kat jayenge.

Part-A: Hindi Efficiency Test (MS Word 2013) Important Topics

Official screenshot mein dikhaya gaya hai ki aapko MS Word 2013 mein 4-5 paragraphs aur neeche ek Table pehle se type ki hui milegi. Aapko 15 questions mein in topics par command lagani hogi:

  • Paragraph Formatting (Home & Page Layout Tab): First Line Indent, Hanging Indent, Left/Right Indentation, Line Spacing (1.15, 1.5, Double, Exactly), Space Before/After Paragraph, aur Alignment (Left, Center, Right, Justify).
  • Font Formatting (Home Tab): Specific paragraph ya shabd ko Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, Double Strikethrough, Superscript, Subscript, Font Size badalna, Text Highlight Color, aur Font Color lagana.
  • Find & Replace (Home ➔ Editing): Kisi vishesh shabd ko dhoondh kar (Find) doosre shabd se Replace karna या uski formatting (jaise Bold) badalna.
  • Page Setup & Design (Page Layout & Design Tab): Page Margins (Top, Bottom, Left, Right), Orientation (Portrait/Landscape), Page Size (A4/Letter), Page Borders, Watermark (Text Watermark), aur Page Color/Background.
  • Header, Footer & Paging (Insert Tab): Header aur Footer insert karna, unka alignment set karna, aur Page Numbers lagana.
  • MS Word Table Tasks: Table ke andar nayi Row/Column insert karna, Cells ko Merge ya Split karna, Table Border style/width badalna, Cell Shading (Background color) dena, aur Table ke andar ke data ko Align karna.

Part-B: English Efficiency Test (MS Excel 2013) Important Topics

Official notification ke Page 6 par MS Excel 2013 ka screenshot diya gaya hai (jisme Train No, State, City, Distance, Time(hrs) ke columns बने hue hain). MS Excel 2013 mein 25/25 laane ke liye in 15 types ke tasks ki practice zaroor karein:

  • Basic Formulas & Calculations: =SUM(), =AVERAGE(), =MAX(), =MIN(), =COUNT(), Multiplication, Division (jaise Distance aur Time se Speed nikalna), aur Percentage calculate karna.
  • Cell Formatting & Alignment (Home Tab): Merge & Center (Title heading ke liye), Wrap Text, Text Orientation (Angle Counterclockwise / Vertical Text), Horizontal aur Vertical Alignment (Top, Middle, Bottom align).
  • Row & Column Dimensions (Home ➔ Cells ➔ Format): Specific Row Height aur Column Width set karna, AutoFit Row Height/Column Width.
  • Number Formatting: Numbers ko Currency, Percentage (%), Comma Style, ya Decimal places (Increase/Decrease Decimal) mein set karna.
  • Borders, Fill & Conditional Formatting: Selected table range par All Borders / Thick Box Border lagana, Cell Background (Fill Color) aur Font Color badalna, aur Conditional Formatting (Highlight Cells Rules - Greater Than, Less Than, Duplicate Values) apply karna.
  • Sorting & Page Setup: Data ko Ascending (A to Z) ya Descending (Z to A) Sort karna, aur Page Layout mein Margins/Orientation set karna.

7. 60-Day Actionable Preparation Plan (Oct 25 to Dec 20, 2026)

Aaj 25 October 2026 hai aur aapka exam 21 December 2026 se shuru hai. Aapke paas lagbhag 55 se 60 din ka golden time bacha hai. Agar aap rozana 3 se 4 ghante neeche diye gaye schedule ko follow karte hain, toh aapko 90+ marks laane se koi nahi rok sakta:

Phase 1: Days 1 to 20 (Accuracy & Spacebar-Lock Conditioning)

  • Stop Pressing Backspace After Space: Apni aadat badlein! Agar koi shabd galat ho raha hai, toh Spacebar dabane se pehle hi use Backspace se theek karein. Agar Spacebar dab gaya, toh us shabd ko bhool jayein aur aage badhein.
  • Zero Invisible Matras: DevLys 010 mein practice karte waqt dhyan rakhein ki chhoti/badi matra ya halant ki key do baar na dabe.
  • Ergonomic Setup: Roz 3-4 ghante bina thake practice karne ke liye sahi posture bahut zaroori hai. Agar aap laptop par practice kar rahe hain ya kamar dard hota hai, toh hamare Best Laptops for Typists aur Best Ergonomic Chairs Guide ko padh kar apna study desk set karein.

Phase 2: Days 21 to 40 (The 400 / 500 Words Marathon)

  • Ab apna target set karein: Hindi mein 10 minute mein 400 Words aur English mein 10 minute mein 500 Words poore karne hain.
  • Rozana subah 1 ghanta Hindi Speed (Learn Hindi Typing) aur shaam ko 1 ghanta English Speed (Learn English Typing) ko dein.
  • Din mein kam se kam 4 full-length 10-minute mock tests hamare RSSB Typing Simulator par dein aur apna error review check karein.

Phase 3: Days 41 to 60 (MS Word & Excel Ribbon Mastery + Exam Hall Simulation)

  • Apne PC mein MS Office 2013 install karein. Mouse ke Right-Click ko bhool jayein aur bina kisi keyboard shortcut ke, sirf upar ke Ribbon Tabs (Home, Insert, Page Layout, Design, Review) se 10 minute mein 15 questions solve karne ki practice karein.
  • December ke first week mein jab RSSB Official Website par mock link aaye, toh us par कम से कम 5-10 baar practice karein.
  • Ghar par practice karte waqt aas-paas thoda shor (keyboard clacking sound) chala kar practice karein taaki exam hall mein 100 bacho ke ek saath typing karne ki aawaz se aapka concentration na toote.

Frequently Asked Questions (RSSB LDC Phase-II 2026)

1. RSSB LDC / Junior Assistant 2026 Typing Test kab hoga?

Official notification ke mutabik, Phase-II Typing aur Efficiency Test online mode mein 21, 22, 23, 24, 26, 28, aur 29 December 2026 ko aayojit kiya jayega. E-Admit card www.rssb.rajasthan.gov.in se download kiye ja sakenge.

2. RSSB Typing Test mein marks kaise calculate hote hain?

English Speed Test mein 500 words ka paragraph aata hai jisme har sahi word ke 0.05 marks milte hain (500 × 0.05 = 25 Marks). Hindi Speed Test mein 400 words ka paragraph aata hai jisme har sahi word ke 0.0625 marks milte hain (400 × 0.0625 = 25 Marks). Galat ya chhoote hue words ke koi marks nahi milte.

3. Kya is baar English Efficiency Test MS Excel mein hoga?

Haan! RSSB ke official notification (Section 4-ख) mein saaf likha hai ki Hindi Efficiency Test MS Word 2013 mein aur English Efficiency Test MS Excel 2013 mein karwaya jayega. Dono mein 15-15 questions honge aur 10-10 minute ka samay milega.

4. Speed Test aur Efficiency Test mein Backspace aur Ctrl+Z ka kya rule hai?

Speed Test mein Backspace tabhi tak kaam karta hai jab tak aapne Spacebar ya Enter nahi dabaya hai. Spacebar dabate hi pichhla word lock ho jata hai. Wahin Efficiency Test mein aap zaroorat ke anusaar Backspace aur CTRL+Z (Undo) ka upyog kar sakte hain, lekin Cut-Copy-Paste, Right-Click aur Shortcut keys band rahengi.

5. Hindi Typing ke liye kaunsa font aur keyboard layout use hoga?

Hindi Typing ke liye Standard Remington Keyboard layout aur DevLys 010 font ka prayog kiya jayega. Dhyan rahe ki exam ke dauran Alt aur Ctrl key ka prayog mana hai. Aap RSSB Typing Simulator par DevLys 010 mein abhi practice kar sakte hain.
