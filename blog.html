Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which Hindi font is used in Govt Typing Exams like SSC, RSSB, HARTRON & Beltron?

Central exams like SSC and Bihar Beltron use Mangal Unicode (Remington Gail or Inscript). Rajasthan RSSB LDC uses DevLys 010, Haryana HARTRON SETC uses Kruti Dev 010, and UPSSSC offers a choice between Kruti Dev 010 and Mangal.

2. What is the official 2026 RSSB LDC Phase-II Typing & Efficiency pattern?

RSSB LDC Phase-II has four 10-minute papers of 25 marks each (minimum 9/25 marks to qualify; 5% SC/ST relaxation): Hindi Speed (DevLys 010), English Speed, Hindi Efficiency (MS Word 2013, 15 tasks), and English Efficiency (MS Excel 2013, 15 tasks).

3. Can I practice typing on a mobile phone without a PC?

Yes. Connect any standard USB computer keyboard to your Android smartphone using a Type-C or Micro-USB OTG adapter and practice Hindi or English typing directly in your mobile browser.

4. How are typing mistakes and Net WPM calculated in government exams?

Most exams count 5 keystrokes as 1 standard word. HARTRON SETC deducts 1 word per mistake divided by 5 minutes, while SSC calculates an Error Percentage using Full Mistakes (omissions, substitutions) and Half Mistakes (spacing, capitalization, punctuation).

5. How long does it take to jump from 20 WPM to 40+ WPM?

With 30 to 45 minutes of daily touch-typing practice focusing on home-row finger placement and 95%+ accuracy before chasing speed, most students reach 35–45 WPM within 30 days.
