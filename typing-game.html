How to Play & Hand Placement

  • 🧘
    Posture & Position: Sit up straight with your feet flat on the floor. Place your left-hand fingers on the keys A, S, D, F and your right-hand fingers lightly on J, K, L, ;. Your thumbs should rest naturally on the Spacebar.
  • 🙈
    The Golden Rule: DO NOT LOOK AT THE KEYBOARD! Keep your eyes glued to the screen. If you make a mistake, let it happen. Forcing yourself to look at the screen builds the crucial "Touch Typing" muscle memory required for exams.

Game Levels & Rules

  • 🚀
    10 Progressive Levels: The game features 10 distinct levels. Levels 1 to 3 teach specific keyboard rows (Home, Top, and Bottom). As you advance, you will face numbers, complex symbols, and eventually the entire keyboard. Level 10 is reserved for advanced, elite-speed typists!
  • 💥
    Level Up & Game Over: For every correct key you hit, you earn points. Score 100 points to automatically Level Up. But be careful—if you skip a key and let 10 falling asteroids hit the red laser line, it's Game Over!

The Perfect Warm-Up for Government Exams

If you are preparing for a serious government typing skill test like SSC CGL, CHSL, Rajasthan LDC, Informatics Assistant (IA), or Bihar Beltron, your fingers need to be properly warmed up before you take a mock test. Professional typists and Data Entry Operators (DEOs) often play 5 to 10 minutes of typing games to get their hands moving quickly and reduce early mistakes.

Start your daily practice session here! Destroy the asteroids, hit Level 10, and once your fingers feel fast and loose, head over to our Strict-Mode Typing Speed Test to evaluate your true exam readiness.
