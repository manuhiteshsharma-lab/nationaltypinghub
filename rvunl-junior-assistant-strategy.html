Rajasthan Power Sector Official Recruitment Guide

RVUNL Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II Strategy 2026: Complete Written CBT & Phase-II Typing Test Master Guide

Based on Advertisement No. RVUN/Rectt.-2026-27/03 • Official Notification Reference • 15 Min Read

The Government of Rajasthan, through its wholly-owned five state power utilities—Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Ltd. (RVUNL), Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Prasaran Nigam Ltd. (RVPNL), Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Ltd. (JVVNL), Ajmer Vidyut Vitran Nigam Ltd. (AVVNL), and Jodhpur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Ltd. (JdVVNL)—has announced a Common Recruitment Exercise under Advertisement No. RVUN/Rectt.-2026-27/03 (Dated August 04, 2026).

This common recruitment exercise targets premier ministerial and commercial positions across state generation, transmission, and distribution companies, including Junior Accountant and Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II. Online applications are accepted from August 05, 2026 to August 25, 2026 (12.00 Midnight).

📌 Important Notification Summary: Selection for Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II is executed in two phases: Phase-I Computer-Based Test (CBT) and Phase-II Typewriting & Efficiency Test. Final merit gives a massive 60% weightage to the Phase-II Typing & Efficiency Test and 40% weightage to Stage-2 Main CBT exam!

1. Power Companies & Field Operations Overview

The 5 state power companies operate across the entirety of Rajasthan and Chhattisgarh (for coal mining), providing 24x7 electricity. Candidates must submit company priorities/preferences at the time of initial application, which remain final and non-alterable.

Power Company Field / Nature of Work Area of Operation & Districts Covered
Raj. Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Ltd. (RVUN) Electricity Generation Main Power Plants at Kota, Suratgarh, Chhabra (Baran), Jhalawar, Ramgarh (Jaisalmer), Banswara, Dholpur, Barmer, & Ambikapur (Chhattisgarh coal mining site).
Raj. Rajya Vidyut Prasaran Nigam Ltd. (RVPN) Electricity Transmission Entire State of Rajasthan.
Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Ltd. (JVVN) Electricity Distribution Jaipur, Dausa, Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur, Sawai Madhopur, Tonk, Karauli, Kota, Jhalawar, Baran, Deeg, Kotputli-Behror, Khairthal-Tijara & Bundi.
Ajmer Vidyut Vitran Nigam Ltd. (AVVN) Electricity Distribution Ajmer, Banswara, Beawar, Bhilwara, Chittorgarh, Didwana-Kuchaman, Dungarpur, Jhunjhunu, Nagaur, Pratapgarh, Rajsamand, Salumbar, Sikar & Udaipur.
Jodhpur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Ltd. (JdVVN) Electricity Distribution Jodhpur, Phalodi, Pali, Sirohi, Barmer, Jaisalmer, Sriganganagar, Balotra, Jalore, Bikaner, Hanumangarh & Churu.

2. Pay Matrix, Remuneration & Probation Terms

Selected candidates are engaged initially as "Probationer Trainee" for a 2-year period with fixed monthly remuneration. Upon successful completion, they are fixed at the minimum basic pay level in the regular pay matrix plus applicable government allowances.

Name of Post Pay Matrix Level Fixed Remuneration (2-Yr Probation) Minimum Basic Pay (Post Probation)

3. Educational Qualification & Computer Certification Criteria

Candidates must possess the prescribed educational and computer qualifications on or before the date fixed for Document Verification after the online competitive examination.

For Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II:

  • Must have passed Senior Secondary (10+2) Examination from a recognized Board or its equivalent examination.
  • AND must possess any ONE of the following computer qualifications:
    • "O" or Higher Level Certificate Course conducted by DOEACC / NIELIT, New Delhi.
    • Certificate course on Computer Concept by NIELIT, New Delhi.
    • COPA (Computer Operator & Programming Assistant) / DPCS (Data Preparation and Computer Software) certificate under NCVT / SCVT.
    • Degree / Diploma / Certificate in Computer Science / Computer Application from a recognized University / Government Institution.
    • Senior Secondary Certificate with Computer Science / Computer Application as one of the subjects.
    • One computer-related subject in one semester/year in Bachelor's Degree / Higher Qualification / CA (I.T.T. Certificate) / ICWA / CS / MBA.
    • Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering from a polytechnic institution recognized by the Government.
    • RSCIT (Rajasthan State Certificate Course in Information Technology) conducted by Vardhaman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota under RKCL.
  • Mandatory Language & Culture Knowledge: Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture.

4. Age Criteria, 3-Year Relaxation & Category Extensions

Candidates must have attained the age of 18 years and must not have attained the age of 40 years as on 1st January next following the application deadline. However, because direct recruitment was not conducted in the previous three years, the upper age limit as on 01.01.2027 is relaxed to 43 years for all candidates.

5. Selection Process & Phase-I CBT Exam Pattern

Selection for Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II is structured across two phases:

Phase-I: Computer Based Test (CBT)

Phase-I comprises two stages: Stage-1 Pre-examination (Screening) and Stage-2 Main-examination. Pre-examination is purely for shortlisting candidates (about 10 times the number of vacancies) and carries no weightage in final merit. Final selection weightage is split as:

  • Phase-I Main Examination CBT: 40% Weightage
  • Phase-II Typewriting & Efficiency Test: 60% Weightage

Phase-I Written Syllabus Breakdown (Class-10th Standard):

  1. Reasoning & Mental Ability: Analytical Reasoning, Number series, Letter series, Odd man out, Coding-Decoding, Shapes & Mirror Images, Clocks, etc.
  2. Mathematics: Class-X level arithmetic and algebra.
  3. General Knowledge & Everyday Science: Current events of State (Rajasthan), National & International importance, Persons & Places in news, Games & Sports, Science, Indian History, Civics, Geography, Polity—with special reference to Rajasthan.
  4. Hindi General: Class-X level Hindi Grammar and language.
  5. English General: Class-X level English Grammar and language.

6. Phase-II Typewriting & Efficiency Test Rules

Candidates equal to about 3 times the category-wise vacancies are admitted to Phase-II based on Main CBT performance. Phase-II carries 100 Total Marks split equally between Hindi and English tests.

8. Computer Efficiency Test (MS Word & MS Excel Strategy)

The 50-mark Efficiency Test evaluates practical document formatting and spreadsheet handling skills on live software terminals.

Core MS Word Formatting Tasks:

  • Paragraph alignment (Left, Right, Center, Justify), Line Spacing (1.5, Double), and First-Line Indentation.
  • Font styling: Bold, Italic, Underline, Font Size, Font Color, and Text Highlight Color.
  • Header & Footer insertion, Page Borders, Shading, Watermark, and Page Numbering.
  • Table formatting: Cell merging, splitting, row height adjustments, and table border styling.

Core MS Excel Formatting & Formula Tasks:

  • Executing basic mathematical formulas: =SUM(), =AVERAGE(), =COUNT(), =MIN(), =MAX().
  • Cell formatting: Currency/Percentage formatting, Text Wrapping, Merge & Center, Border styles.
  • Gridline visibility, Column width auto-fit, and sorting table rows alphabetically/numerically.

9. Document Verification Checklist & Scanning Specifications

Candidates invited for Document Verification (about 2 times the total vacancies) must present original credentials along with two sets of self-attested photocopies.

Document Checklist:

  1. System-generated application printout with scanned photo and signature.
  2. Printout of fee payment E-Receipt.
  3. 10th / Secondary Board Certificate & Marksheet showing Date of Birth.
  4. 12th / Senior Secondary Board Certificate & Marksheet.
  5. Graduation / Degree / Diploma Marksheets and Certificates.
  6. Prescribed Computer Qualification Certificate (RSCIT / O-Level / COPA / Degree).
  7. Valid Category Caste Certificate (SC/ST/BC/MBC Non-Creamy Layer / EWS Income & Assets Certificate issued within 12 months).
  8. Domicile / Bonafide Resident Certificate of Rajasthan State ("Vishesh Mool Niwas" for TSP areas).
  9. Good Character Certificate from last educational institute and two character certificates from responsible citizens (issued within last 6 months).
  10. Affidavit regarding Children (No more than 2 children born after 01.06.2002), Marriage Certificate (if married), and Non-Criminal Case Affidavit on Rs. 50/- Stamp Paper.

10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) & Official Portal Links

Q1: When does the online registration start and end?

Answer: Applications open on August 5, 2026 (10.00 AM) and close on August 25, 2026 (12.00 Midnight).

Q2: What is the application fee structure?

Answer: Unreserved (UR / General) candidates pay Rs. 1,000/-. Reserved candidates (SC/ST/BC/MBC/EWS/PwBD of Rajasthan) pay Rs. 500/-. Candidates from states other than Rajasthan apply under UR General category paying Rs. 1,000/-.

Q3: What are the examination cities proposed in Rajasthan?

Answer: Computer-based test centers are proposed at Ajmer, Alwar, Bharatpur, Bikaner, Dausa, Hanumangarh, Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, Sikar, Sriganganagar, and Udaipur.

Q4: Is negative marking applicable in the exam?

Answer: For Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II, there is NO negative marking in Stage-1 Pre-examination. However, negative marking is applicable in the Stage-2 Main Examination.

Q5: What is the official helpline number for recruitment queries?

Answer: Official Helpline Phone: +91 94140 56655 (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days).
