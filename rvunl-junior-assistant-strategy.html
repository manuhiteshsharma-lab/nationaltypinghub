Rajasthan Power Sector Official Recruitment Guide

RVUNL Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II Strategy 2026: Complete Written CBT & Phase-II Typing Test Master Guide

Updated for 2026 Recruitment • 12 Min Read • By National Typing Hub Team

The Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited (RVUNL) has opened online applications for 765 Junior Assistant and Commercial Assistant-II vacancies across state power companies (RVUNL, RVPNL, JVVNL, AVVNL, and JDVVNL). This is one of Rajasthan's most sought-after clerical recruitments.

Whether you are an absolute beginner touching the keyboard for the first time, or an advanced typist looking to polish your MS Word efficiency skills, this guide is designed to take you step-by-step from day one of your preparation all the way to final selection.

📌 Important Notification Summary: Selection for Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II is executed in two phases: Phase-I Computer-Based Test (CBT) and Phase-II Typewriting & Efficiency Test. Final merit gives a massive 60% weightage to the Phase-II Typing & Efficiency Test and 40% weightage to Stage-2 Main CBT exam!

1. Educational Qualification & Computer Certification Criteria

Candidates must possess the prescribed educational and computer qualifications on or before the date fixed for Document Verification after the online competitive examination.

For Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II:

  • Must have passed Senior Secondary (10+2) Examination from a recognized Board or its equivalent examination.
  • AND must possess any ONE of the following computer qualifications:
    • "O" or Higher Level Certificate Course conducted by DOEACC / NIELIT, New Delhi.
    • Certificate course on Computer Concept by NIELIT, New Delhi.
    • COPA (Computer Operator & Programming Assistant) / DPCS (Data Preparation and Computer Software) certificate.
    • Degree / Diploma / Certificate in Computer Science / Computer Application from a recognized University.
    • Senior Secondary Certificate with Computer Science / Computer Application as one of the subjects.
    • RSCIT (Rajasthan State Certificate Course in Information Technology) conducted by Vardhaman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota.
  • Mandatory Language Knowledge: Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture.

2. Selection Process & Phase-I CBT Exam Pattern

Selection for Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II is structured across two phases:

Phase-I: Computer Based Test (CBT)

Phase-I comprises two stages: Stage-1 Pre-examination (Screening) and Stage-2 Main-examination. Pre-examination is purely for shortlisting candidates (about 10 times the number of vacancies) and carries no weightage in final merit.

Phase-I Written Syllabus Breakdown (Class-10th Standard):

  1. Reasoning & Mental Ability: Analytical Reasoning, Number series, Coding-Decoding, Shapes & Mirror Images.
  2. Mathematics: Class-X level arithmetic and algebra.
  3. General Knowledge & Everyday Science: Current events, Persons & Places in news, Games & Sports, Science, Indian History, Civics, Geography—with special reference to Rajasthan.
  4. Hindi General: Class-X level Hindi Grammar and language.
  5. English General: Class-X level English Grammar and language.

Strategy Tip: Do not treat Computer Science solely as a written subject. Understanding MS Word and MS Excel ribbons now will make your Phase II Efficiency Test effortless.

Candidates equal to about 3 times the category-wise vacancies are admitted to Phase-II based on Main CBT performance. Phase-II carries 100 Total Marks split equally between Hindi and English tests.

Stage 1: Muscle Memory (Beginners)

If you are a beginner, do not attempt full-paragraph mock tests right away. Focus purely on touch typing. Place your fingers on the Home Row (A S D F and J K L ; for English / क त च ह mapping for Devlys 010). Practice typing without looking down at the keyboard keys.

Stage 2: Mastering Backspace Management

During official exams, excessive backspacing drains time. Practice typing in our simulators by enabling the Strict Mode to build confidence in typing words correctly on the first attempt without relying on corrections.

5. Computer Efficiency Test (MS Word & MS Excel Strategy)

The 50-mark Efficiency Test evaluates practical document formatting and spreadsheet handling skills on live software terminals. You will be given a printed sheet containing formatting instructions to perform within 10 minutes per language.

Core MS Word Formatting Tasks:

  • Paragraphs: Alignment (Left, Right, Center, Justify), Line Spacing (1.5, Double), and First-Line Indentation.
  • Font Styling: Bold, Italic, Underline, Font Size, Font Color, and Text Highlight Color.
  • Page Layout: Header & Footer insertion, Page Borders, Shading, Watermark, and Page Numbering.
  • Tables: Cell merging, splitting, row height adjustments, and table border styling.

Core MS Excel Formatting & Formula Tasks:

  • Executing basic mathematical formulas: =SUM(), =AVERAGE(), =COUNT(), =MIN(), =MAX().
  • Cell formatting: Currency/Percentage formatting, Text Wrapping, Merge & Center, Border styles.
  • Gridline visibility, Column width auto-fit, and sorting table rows alphabetically/numerically.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When does the online registration start and end?

Answer: Applications typically run for a 20-day window following the official advertisement release. Ensure you verify the exact opening and closing dates via the official energy portal.

Q2: Can I use Mangal font for the Hindi typing test?

Answer: No. The official notification explicitly requires the use of Devnagari script typing in Kruti Dev / Devlys 010 fonts. Mangal (Inscript) layout is not permitted.

Q3: What is the application fee structure?

Answer: Unreserved (UR / General) candidates usually pay Rs. 1,000/-. Reserved candidates (SC/ST/BC/MBC/EWS/PwBD of Rajasthan) pay Rs. 500/-. Candidates from states other than Rajasthan apply under the UR General category.

Q4: Is negative marking applicable in the exam?

Answer: For Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II, there is NO negative marking in Stage-1 Pre-examination. However, negative marking is applicable in the Stage-2 Main Examination.
