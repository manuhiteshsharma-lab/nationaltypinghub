RVUNL Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II 2026: Complete Written & Typing Strategy (765 Posts)

Updated for 2026 Recruitment • 8 Min Read • By National Typing Hub Team

The Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited (RVUNL) has opened online applications for 765 Junior Assistant and Commercial Assistant-II vacancies across state power companies (RVUNL, RVPNL, JVVNL, AVVNL, and JDVVNL). This is one of Rajasthan's most sought-after clerical recruitments.

Selection is divided into two distinct phases: Phase I (Phase 1 Written Examination) and Phase II (Computer Typing & Efficiency Test). To help both absolute beginners and experienced aspirants, we have broken down the entire preparation roadmap into two actionable strategies.

Strategy 1: How to Master the Phase 1 Written Exam

The Phase 1 Written Exam is a competitive computer-based test (CBT). Depending on whether you belong to the commerce or general non-technical stream, the paper structure focuses heavily on speed and accuracy.

Subject / Section Key Focus Areas Preparation Priority
Reasoning & Mental Ability Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations High (Daily Practice)

3-Step Written Exam Roadmap:

  1. Fix Rajasthan GK First: Over 40% of the General Awareness weightage directly highlights Rajasthan's geography, history, and energy projects. Read standard state board textbooks or dedicated state GK manuals.
  2. Daily Reasoning Time-Table: Solve at least 30-40 reasoning questions daily under timed conditions to improve speed for the online CBT interface.
  3. Master MS Office Basics Early: Do not treat Computer Science solely as a written subject. Understanding MS Word and MS Excel ribbons now will make your Phase II Efficiency Test effortless.

Strategy 2: Step-by-Step Guide for Phase 2 Typing & Efficiency Test

Phase II carries 100 Marks and consists of two separate sub-tests conducted on computers:

  • English Typing Speed Test: 15 Minutes (25 Marks)
  • Hindi Typing Speed Test: 15 Minutes (25 Marks)
  • Computer Efficiency Test: 15 Minutes (50 Marks)

⚠️ Official Rule: The Hindi Typing test is conducted exclusively in Devlys 010 (or Kruti Dev 010) font on a Remington typewriter layout. Unicode fonts like Mangal or Inscript are NOT permitted for RVUNL typing.

Step-by-Step Typing Mastery (From Beginner to Expert):

Stage 1: Muscle Memory (Days 1 to 7)

If you are a beginner, do not attempt full-paragraph mock tests right away. Focus purely on touch typing. Place your fingers on the Home Row (A S D F and J K L ; for English / क त च ह mapping for Devlys 010). Practice typing without looking down at the keyboard keys.

Stage 2: Handling Alt Codes & Alt Combinations (Days 8 to 15)

Devlys 010 requires typing specific numerical Alt Codes for half-letters and special characters (e.g., Alt + 0216 for क्र, Alt + 0170 for ्र). Create a cheat sheet for the top 15 mandatory Alt Codes required in Rajasthan exams and practice them for 20 minutes every morning.

Stage 3: Handling Restricted Backspace & Spacebar Locks (Days 16 to 30)

During official exams, excessive backspacing drains time. Practice typing in simulators that enforce restricted backspacing so you learn to get words right on the first attempt.

How to Clear the 50-Mark Efficiency Test:

The Efficiency Test evaluates your practical knowledge of MS Word and MS Excel. You will be given a printed sheet containing formatting instructions to perform on a given document within 15 minutes.

  • MS Word Focus: Paragraph indentation, font size/color changes, header/footer insertion, table creation, line spacing, border shading, and page setup.
  • MS Excel Focus: Basic formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT), cell formatting, text wrapping, sorting data, merging cells, and gridline options.
