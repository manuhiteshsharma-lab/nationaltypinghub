HARTRON SETC Exam & Typing Test Guide 2026: Official Formula, Online Apply, Syllabus & Tricks to Pass in First Attempt

📅 Updated: October 25, 2026 ⏱️ Read Time: 14 Mins 🏛️ Authority: HARTRON (Govt. of Haryana)

Ram-Ram Haryana ke sabhi Government Employees aur Aspirants! Agar aap Haryana Government ke kisi bhi Department, Board, Corporation ya Agency mein Clerk, Data Entry Operator (DEO), Assistant, Steno-typist ya Group-D Employee ke pad par kaam kar rahe hain (chahe regular ho ya contractual), toh aapke career ki sabse badi chabi hai—SETC (State Eligibility Test in Computer Appreciation & Applications). Bina SETC clear kiye na toh aapko apke pay scale mein Annual Increment milega aur na hi Promotion!

Haryana State Electronics Development Corporation Limited (HARTRON) dwara jari kiye gaye latest 2026 Prospectus-cum-Guidelines mein kuch bahut bade updates aaye hain. Khaas taur par 03 June 2026 ke Gazette Notification (No. 85-2026/Ext.) ke baad Common-Cadre Group-D employees ke promotion rules, aur 2025 ke memos ke baad Sub-Inspector (Co-Operation Dept), Divisional Accountant aur Assistant Lineman (Energy Dept) ki entry ne SETC ko aur bhi mahatvapurna bana diya hai.

Haryana Sarkar ke Notification No. 42/164/2008-3GS-II (dated 07.11.2013) aur instruction dated 17.11.2018 ke tahat, sarkar ka uddeshya (object) har government office mein 100% IT Literacy laana aur e-Governance projects ko badhava dena hai. Isliye sabhi working Clerks aur allied cadres ke liye SETC pass karna 100% Mandatory hai.

3. SETC Part-II Typing Speed Calculation Formula (असली गणित समझें)

HARTRON SETC Prospectus ke Section 3.0 mein typing speed calculate karne ka official mathematical formula diya gaya hai. Bahut saare karmchari (employees) yeh nahi samajh paate ki HARTRON shabdon (words) ko kaise ginta hai aur galti (mistake) katne ka kya asar padta hai. Chaliye isko ekdam aasan bhasha mein decode karte hain:

Official HARTRON Mathematical Rules:

  • Rule 1 (5 Keystrokes = 1 Word): HARTRON actual dictionary words nahi ginta, balki Key Depressions (Strokes) ginta hai! Aapke dwara dabaye gaye har 5 Strokes (including spaces) ko 1 Word maana jata hai.
  • Rule 2 (Total Words Typed): 5 minute mein aapne jitne कुल strokes type kiye, unhe 5 se bhag (divide) karne par aapke Total Words Typed nikalte hain.
  • Rule 3 (Gross Speed in WPM): Total Words Typed ko test ke samay (5 Minutes) se divide karne par aapki Gross Speed (WPM) aati hai.
  • Rule 4 (Mistake Penalty): Aapki kul galtiyon (Total Mistakes) ko 5 se divide kiya jata hai (Mistakes = Total Mistakes / 5) aur use Gross Speed mein se ghata (subtract) diya jata hai! Iska matlab hai ki 1 Galti aapke 1 poore Word (5 keystrokes) ko kha jaati hai!
📐 Official HARTRON SETC Net WPM Formula: Total Words = Total Strokes / 5 Gross WPM = Total Words / 5 (Minutes) Net Typing Speed (WPM) = Gross WPM - (Total Mistakes / 5) Shortcut Formula: Net WPM = (Total Words Typed - Total Mistakes) / 5

Official Prospectus Solved Example (Step-by-Step):

Maan lijiye ek candidate ne 5 Minutes mein 1200 Strokes type kiye aur usse 10 Mistakes (ग़लतियाँ) ho gayin. Toh HARTRON ka software uski speed aise nikalega:

  1. Step 1 (Total Words): 1200 Strokes ÷ 5 = 240 Words
  2. Step 2 (Gross Typing Speed): 240 Words ÷ 5 Minutes = 48 Gross WPM
  3. Step 3 (Mistake Deduction): 10 Mistakes ÷ 5 = 2 WPM Penalty
  4. Step 4 (Final Net Typing Speed): 48 Gross WPM - 2 Penalty = 46 Net WPM (Ya seedhe: (240 - 10) ÷ 5 = 46 WPM).

4. How to Apply Online for HARTRON SETC & Document Verification Rules

Bahut saare employees test mein pass hone ke baawajood document verification mein बाहर (reject) kar diye jaate hain kyunki unhone form ke printout par apne office se sahi tarike se attestation aur DAK/Dispatch Number nahi lagwaya hota. Official Prospectus ke Section 7.0, 8.0, 9.0 aur 13.0 ko dhyan se follow karein:

1

Visit Official Portal

Sirf official website hartron.org.in par jaakar online form bharein. Typing language (English/Hindi/Both) dhyan se chunein.

2

Save, Preview & Edit

'Save & Preview' par click karke apni details check karein. Fee katne se pehle 'Edit/Update' kar sakte hain, fee ke baad koi correction nahi hoga!

3

Pay Exam Fee Online

₹590/- (For Both Part-I & II) ya ₹354/- (Only Part-II for Exempted candidates) online जमा karein.

4

Office Stamp & DAK No.

Form ka printout nikal kar apne office ke Competent Authority se sign, stamp, photo attest aur DAK/Dispatch No. with Date zaroor dalwayein!

💰 Fee Structure & Payment Modes (Section 7.0 & 11.0)

  • Non-Exempted Applicants (Part-I + Part-II Both): ₹590/- + Bank Charges.
  • Exempted Applicants (Only Part-II Typing Test): ₹354/- + Bank Charges.
  • Bank Charges Note: Debit Card: NIL, Credit Card: NIL, Internet Banking: Approx. ₹10 to ₹12/-. (Application fee non-refundable hai).
  • Warning on Multiple Forms: Ek candidate koek hi application form bharne ki anumati hai. Ek se zyada form bharne par aapki candidature seedhe reject kar di jayegi!

⚠️ MANDATORY CHECKLIST FOR EXAM CENTRE ENTRY (इसे भूलकर भी मिस न करें!)

Prospectus ke Point 9(c) aur 13(vi) mein saaf likha hai ki aapko Application Form डाक (Post/Courier) se HARTRON ko NAHI bhejna hai! Aapko yeh saare documents exam wale din 30 minute pehle apne saath Exam Centre par lekar aane hain:

  2. Attested Latest Photograph: Application form par jo aapki latest printed photo hai, woh bhi aapke Department ke Competent Authority dwara duly attested (मोहर और हस्ताक्षर सहित) honi chahiye.
  3. e-Admit Card Printout: HARTRON kisi ko alag se post ya SMS nahi bhejta. Aapko hartron.org.in se apna e-Admit card download karke laana hoga.

  5. Exemption / Disability Certificate (If applicable): Agar aapne Part-I se exemption liya hai, toh us computer course/degree ka Original Certificate + Self-attested photocopy saath laana anivarya hai. Benchmark Disabled (40%+ disability) candidates ko apna original Disability Certificate dikhana hoga.

5. Complete SETC Part-I Syllabus (Annexure 'A' - 8 Modules Breakdown)

Jin employees ke paas Part-I exemption certificate nahi hai, ya jo Group-D aur Skill Development Department ke karmchari hain, unhe Part-I pass karna behad zaroori hai. Isme 50 Objective MCQs (sirf English bhasha mein) aate hain aur pass hone ke liye 50% (25 Marks) laane hote hain. Official Annexure 'A' ke 8 modules ka nichod yahan hai:

1. Introduction to Computer

History & Generations of Computers, e-Governance applications, CPU, VDU, Input/Output devices, Primary & Secondary Memory (RAM, ROM, Memory units), Connecting peripherals, Left/Right Mouse button functions, Hardware vs System & Application Software.

2. Introduction to Windows

Operating System basics, User Interface, My Computer, Taskbar, Status Bar, Recycle Bin, Windows Explorer (Creating, deleting, renaming files/folders), Control Panel, Display Properties, Creating Shortcuts, Notepad, Sound Recorder & Media Player.

3. Word Processing (MS Word)

Menu & Toolbars, Save vs Save As, Page Setup, Print Preview, Cut/Copy/Paste, Undo/Redo, Find & Replace, Thesaurus, Font/Size, Alignment, Bullets, Change Case, Tabs/Indents, Table Manipulation (Merge/Split cells, Borders, Convert table to text) & Mail Merge.

4. Spread-Sheet (MS Excel)

Workbook saving, Cell addressing, Inserting/deleting rows, columns & worksheets, Adjusting cell height/width, Formulas & Functions (SUM, AVERAGE, Percentage), Data Sorting, Subtotals, Applying Filters, Inserting Charts, Daily/Monthly sales reports & budgets.

5. Power-Point

Creating presentations via Auto Content Wizard & Slide Layouts, PowerPoint Views, Slide Master View, Adding Header/Footer, Inserting Organization Charts, Slide Transitions (Timing, Sound), Custom Animations & Drawing Toolbar.

6. Internet Basics

Computer Networks: Local Area Network (LAN) & Wide Area Network (WAN), Concept & Advantages of Internet, Internet Service Providers (ISP), Internet Access methods: Broadband, Dial-Up, Wi-Fi & Basic Troubleshooting.

7. WWW & Web Browsers

World Wide Web (WWW) concepts, Web Browsers (Internet Explorer, Netscape Communicator), URL addresses, Search Engines, Surfing the web, Printing & Saving webpages, Uploading vs Downloading & Online Chatting.

8. Introduction to E-mail

Creating an Email account, Email addressing format, Inbox & Outbox, Creating, Viewing, Sending, Replying & Forwarding emails, Sorting/Searching mails, Sending same mail to multiple users (CC/BCC) & Sending soft copy documents as Attachments.

6. Best Tips & Tricks to Pass SETC Typing Test (Part-II) in First Attempt

HARTRON SETC ka typing test 10 ya 15 minute ka marathon nahi hai—yeh sirf 5 Minute ka T20 Sprint hai! 5 minute mein agar aap shuru ke 1 minute ghabra gaye, toh aapki speed recover nahi ho पाती. In 6 tested tricks ko aaj se hi apni practice mein laayein:

  1. Choose Your Language Wisely (English vs Hindi KrutiDev010): Form bharte waqt HARTRON aapko option deta hai ki aap English (30 WPM), Hindi (25 WPM), ya Both mein se kya chunte hain. Agar aapke department mein kisi ek bhasha se kaam chal jata hai aur aapki English typing achhi hai, toh English (30 WPM) sabse aasan padta hai kyunki isme Shift key aur matraon ka jhanjhat kam hota hai. Agar aap Hindi chunte hain, toh yaad rakhein ki test sirf KRUTIDEV010 font mein hoga!
  2. The First 60 Seconds "Warm-Up" Trick: Exam hall (Gurugram ya Ambala) mein jab 5-minute ka timer start hota hai, toh pehle 45-60 seconds mein apni top speed par mat bhagein! Pehle 1 minute mein 85% speed aur 100% accuracy ke saath apni ungliyon ko keyboard par set hone dein. Jab rhythm ban jaye, tab agle 4 minute mein speed push karein.
  3. Understand the 1 Mistake = 5 Strokes Penalty: Jaisa humne formula mein dekha, aapki 1 galti aapke 5 sahi keystrokes (1 word) ko zero kar deti hai. Isliye galat type karke aage badhne se behtar hai ki aap turant apni accuracy par control rakhein.
  4. Master Kruti Dev 010 Common Words: Agar aap Hindi mein SETC de rahe hain, toh sarkaari patraon (official letters) mein aane wale common words jaise हरियाणा सरकार, निदेशालय, कर्मचारी, वेतनमान, पदोन्नति, कार्यालय, पंचकूला, कृपया, उपरोक्त ki muscle memory bana lein. Agar aapko Unicode se Kruti Dev mein practice paragraphs convert karne hain, toh hamara free Unicode to Kruti Dev Converter use karein.
  5. Practice on Hard Membrane Keyboards: Exam centre par aapko gaming keyboard nahi milega, balki standard office membrane keyboards (jaise HP, Dell, ya TVS Champ) milenge. Ghar par laptop ke flat keyboard par practice karne ke bajaye ek external USB keyboard lagayein. Kaunsa keyboard best hai, yeh jaanne ke liye hamari Best Typing Keyboards Buying Guide dekhein.
  6. Keep 5 WPM Safety Cushion: Agar English mein 30 WPM chahiye, toh ghar par 5-Minute Speed Test mein jab tak aapki 36-38 Net WPM lagataar na aaye, tab tak chain se mat baithein. Exam hall ke AC aur shor mein 3-4 WPM speed naturally drop hoti hai.

Frequently Asked Questions (HARTRON SETC Exam 2026)

1. HARTRON SETC Typing Test mein speed kaise calculate hoti hai?

HARTRON 5 strokes ko 1 word maanta hai aur test 5 minute ka hota hai. Formula hai: Total Words = Total Strokes / 5, Gross WPM = Total Words / 5, aur Net WPM = Gross WPM - (Total Mistakes / 5). Udaharan ke liye, agar aapne 5 minute mein 1200 strokes (240 words) type kiye aur 10 mistakes keen, toh aapki Net Speed (240 - 10) / 5 = 46 WPM hogi.

2. SETC Part-II pass karne ke liye English aur Hindi mein kitni speed chahiye?

English typing test pass karne ke liye minimum 30 Net WPM (kam se kam 750 error-free keystrokes in 5 mins) aur Hindi typing test pass karne ke liye minimum 25 Net WPM (kam se kam 625 error-free keystrokes in 5 mins) chahiye. Hindi typing test strictly KRUTIDEV010 font mein hota hai.

3. Common-Cadre Group-D Employees ke liye June 2026 ka naya SETC rule kya hai?

Haryana Govt Gazette Notification No. 85-2026/Ext. dated 03.06.2026 ke anusaar, Common-Cadre Group-D employees SETC ka Part-I pass karte hi Promotion ke liye eligible ho jaate hain (unhe Part-I pass certificate milta hai). Halaanki, annual increments aur full SETC certificate paane ke liye unhe 1 saal ke andar Part-II (Typing Test) bhi pass karna hota hai.

4. Kya SETC ka Application Form dak (Post) se Panchkula bhejna hota hai?

Bilkul Nahi! Prospectus ke Point 9(c) mein saaf likha hai ki Application Form post ya courier se nahi bhejna hai. Aapko form ka printout nikal kar apne office ke Competent Authority se Verification Section sign/stamp karwana hai (with DAK/Dispatch No. & Date, aur attested photo) aur use exam wale din apne saath Exam Centre (Gurugram ya Ambala) lekar aana hai.

5. Kin employees ko SETC Part-I देने की ज़रूरत नहीं है (Exemption)?

Jin employees ke paas Haryana Govt Notification dated 07.11.2013 (Point 9) aur Order dated 19.09.2019 mein mention kiye gaye recognized Computer Courses/Degrees (jaise B.Tech, MCA, BCA, O-Level, या HARTRON/HKCL diploma as per norms) hain, unhe Part-I se chhoot (exemption) milti hai. Unhe sirf ₹354/- fee dekar Part-II (Typing Test) dena hota hai.
