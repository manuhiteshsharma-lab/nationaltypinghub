How The Standard Typing Formula Works

This page uses the globally accepted standard speed calculation method:

  • Word Definition: 1 Word is strictly calculated as 5 Keystrokes (including spaces).
  • Gross Speed: (Total Keystrokes ÷ 5) ÷ Total Time in Minutes.
  • Net Speed: Gross Speed − (Total Incorrect Words ÷ Total Time in Minutes).
  • Flexibility: Unrestricted Backspace is active, meaning you can fix errors anytime before the timer ends.
