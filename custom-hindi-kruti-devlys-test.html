Why Practice with Custom Kruti Dev Text?

While standard simulators provide random text, many candidates preparing for RSSB LDC, Rajasthan High Court, or MP CPCT have specific PDF notes, previous year question papers, or coaching institute paragraphs they want to practice. This Custom Kruti Dev 010 / DevLys Typing Practice tool allows you to paste your own legacy text and evaluate your typing speed exactly as the government would.

How Custom Typing Formulas Work (RSSB, HARTRON, SSC)

Our multi-formula simulator adapts to your selected exam criteria in real-time:

  • Standard Net WPM: The universal format subtracting mistake penalties from your Gross Word Per Minute speed.
  • RSSB LDC Official (Word-Lock): Emulates the strict Rajasthan RSSB Phase-II pattern where Backspace locks immediately after pressing the Spacebar, and awards 0.0625 marks per correct Hindi word.
  • HARTRON SETC Official (Stroke WPM): Evaluates speed based on the official 1 Word = 5 Strokes definition, dividing total strokes to find Net Speed.
  • SSC CHSL / CGL (KDPH): Tracks Key Depressions Per Hour rather than standard WPM for central government data entry roles.
