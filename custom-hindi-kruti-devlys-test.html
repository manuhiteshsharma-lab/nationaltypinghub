How The Standard Typing Formula Works

  • Word Definition: 1 Word is strictly calculated as 5 Keystrokes (including spaces).
  • Gross Speed (WPM): (Total Keystrokes ÷ 5) ÷ Total Time in Minutes.
  • Net Speed (WPM): Gross Speed − (Total Incorrect Words ÷ Total Time in Minutes).
  • Auto Smart-Quote Sanitizer: Automatically fixes MS Word curly quotes (’, ”) and hidden non-breaking spaces pasted from documents so words like विश्व and विशाल grade accurately.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I use this Custom Kruti Dev / DevLys Test tool?

Click the light-green "Paste Custom Text" button at the top, copy your Kruti Dev 010 or DevLys 010 paragraph from MS Word or a PDF, and paste it into the text box. Select your time limit (2, 5, 10, or 15 minutes) and click "Start Custom Typing Test". If you have Unicode (Mangal) text, convert it first using our free Unicode to Kruti Dev Converter.

2. How is my typing test result calculated?

Your score is evaluated using the Standard Typing Formula: every 5 keystrokes count as 1 word (Total Words = Total Strokes ÷ 5). Your Gross WPM is Total Words divided by elapsed minutes, and your Net WPM deducts 1 word per mistake divided by time. After submitting, click "View Detailed Scorecard & Red Errors" to see every wrong word highlighted in red.

3. What is the maximum word length limit to paste custom text?

You can paste up to 2,000 words in a single custom test session. A live word counter above the paste box tracks your count in real time—enough text even for a full 15-minute high-speed marathon without running out of words.
