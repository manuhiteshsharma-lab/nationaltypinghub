🧭 Who We Are & Why We Built NTH

Every year, millions of candidates across India clear written exams for SSC CGL/CHSL, Rajasthan RSSB LDC & Junior Assistant, Haryana HARTRON SETC, MP CPCT, Bihar Beltron, UPSSSC, RRB NTPC, and State High Courts—only to struggle in the final typing and computer efficiency stage because generic typing sites do not match real exam rules.

National Typing Hub was created to close that gap. Instead of locking exam-accurate software behind monthly coaching subscriptions, we engineered lightweight browser simulators that replicate official Hindi fonts (DevLys 010, Kruti Dev 010, Mangal Remington Gail, and Mangal Inscript), Word-Lock Backspace, Ribbon-only MS Word/Excel 2013 grading, and exact board formulas—accessible on any desktop PC or Android phone via OTG keyboard.

Our Core Mission: Keep every typing simulator, efficiency test, font converter, letter pad, biodata maker, and resume builder 100% free for students—with zero paywalls, zero forced sign-ups, and zero watermarks.
