JSSC Typing Test Rules 2026: Kruti Dev 010 & Exemption Explained

If you are appearing for Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) exams for posts like LDC, Panchayat Secretary, Clerk, or Stenographer, you must clear the computer typing test. The JSSC maintains strict evaluation criteria, specifically regarding font usage and error exemptions.

Official JSSC Exam Parameters

  • Duration: Usually 10 minutes.
  • Hindi Typing Target: 25 WPM minimum speed is required. You must type in the Kruti Dev 010 font on a Remington layout. Mangal font is generally not used in Jharkhand state exams.
  • English Typing Target: 30 WPM minimum speed required (depending on the post).
  • Test Environment: Backspace is strictly disabled in most JSSC skill tests. You must maintain high accuracy to prevent massive deductions from your gross WPM.

💡 The JSSC 2% Mistake Exemption Rule:
In many JSSC exams (like Panchayat Secretary and LDC), the commission allows a 2% mistake exemption. This means if your total mistakes are under 2% of the total words typed, you will not face heavy penalties. However, accuracy is paramount. Mistakes beyond this limit directly reduce your Net WPM.

How to Clear the JSSC Test

Because the Hindi requirement is locked to Kruti Dev 010, the biggest challenge is Accuracy and Font Familiarity. Kruti Dev 010 requires you to memorize complex Alt Codes for special characters.
