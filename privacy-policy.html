🔒 1. Zero-Server Architecture & Local Browser Processing

National Typing Hub (nationaltypinghub.in) is hosted as a static, client-side web platform with no backend user database. Your privacy is built directly into our architecture rather than merely promised in text.

Strict Local-Only Guarantee: When you use our Marriage Biodata Maker (including uploaded profile photos, family details, and contact numbers), Job Resume Maker, Hindi & English Letter Pads, Custom Kruti Dev / DevLys Paste Test, Font Converters, or Typing & MS Word/Excel Efficiency Simulators, all text, photos, and PDF/image exports are processed 100% locally inside your web browser. None of this content is ever transmitted to, logged on, or stored on National Typing Hub servers.

Voice Typing Notice (Hindi Letter Pad): If you voluntarily activate the microphone for Voice Typing, audio recognition is handled directly by your browser's built-in Web Speech API (such as Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge) subject to your browser provider's privacy terms. National Typing Hub does not record, intercept, or store your voice audio.

⚖️ 4. Your Rights Under Indian Law (DPDP Act, 2023) & Children’s Privacy

We respect the rights of all Data Principals under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the DPDP Rules, 2025. Because we do not operate user accounts or store user documents on our servers, you maintain direct control over your data on your own device:

Access, Correction & Erasure

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any voluntary message you sent via our feedback form at any time.

Consent Withdrawal

You may withdraw consent for cookies, analytics, or personalized ads at any time via your browser settings or Google Ads Settings.

Right to Grievance & Nominate

You have the right to lodge a privacy grievance with us (or the Data Protection Board of India) and to nominate a representative as provided under the DPDP Act.

Children’s Privacy (Under 18 Years — India DPDP Act Compliance): Under Section 2(f) and Section 9 of India's DPDP Act, 2023, a child is defined as an individual under 18 years of age. National Typing Hub does not knowingly collect personal identifiable data from individuals under 18 without verifiable parental or lawful guardian consent, and does not engage in behavioral monitoring or targeted profiling of minors. If a parent or guardian believes a minor has voluntarily submitted personal information through our feedback form, please contact us immediately via our Feedback & Support page for prompt erasure.
