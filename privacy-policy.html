Privacy Policy

Data Security During Typing Tests & Conversions

When you use National Typing Hub to practice for government typing exams—such as SSC, UPSSSC, Rajasthan Informatics Assistant (IA), Sanganak (Computor), Tax Assistant, Junior Assistant, Personal Assistant, and Clerk—your data security is our top priority. Whether you are preparing for a Bank Job, training as a Data Entry Operator, or running your own business like an eMitra or Citizen Service Center (CSC), you can confidently convert fonts between Kruti Dev 010 and Unicode (Mangal). We do not store your typed keystrokes, personal practice paragraphs, or font conversion inputs on our backend servers. Your text remains entirely on your local device.

General Privacy Policy

At National Typing Hub, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by National Typing Hub and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us. By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Log Files

National Typing Hub follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes IP addresses, browser type, ISP, date/time stamp, and referring pages. These are not linked to personally identifiable information.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, National Typing Hub uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences. We use local storage to save your language and theme preferences exclusively to enhance your experience.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses DART cookies to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Visitors may decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Note that National Typing Hub has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

Third Party Privacy Policies

National Typing Hub's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

Children's Information

National Typing Hub does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.
